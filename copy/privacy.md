# Your privacy

Looking for a job is personal. Here's exactly what happens to what you paste in — in plain words.

**We don't keep anything you paste.** It's used once, in the moment, to generate your fit check — and then it's gone. We don't save them, we can't pull them up later, and there's no history sitting on a server somewhere.

**No account, no sign-up, no email, no name.** You never hand us your identity. Your browser holds a small random ID so we can remember how many free runs you have left — that's all it's for, and it isn't linked to who you are.

**How the check actually works.** To run the comparison, we send your CV and the job text to Anthropic, the company behind the Claude AI that does the analysis. We don't store either one ourselves.

**We do measure how the site is used** — like which pages get opened and roughly how long a check takes — using privacy-friendly analytics on European servers. It never includes your CV, the job text, or your results, it doesn't use cookies, and it never identifies you.

**In short:** your CV stays yours. We help you hold it up against a job, and then we let it go.
